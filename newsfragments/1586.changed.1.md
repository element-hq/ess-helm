Extend MatrixRTC Redis support to include an external Redis/Valkey with `matrixRTC.redisOrValkey`.

Unlike with other components configuring via `matrixRTC.redis` is not supported as `<component>.redis` is deprecated.

If both the MatrixRTC Authoriser and SFU are configured with an external Redis/Valkey, the in-chart Valkey is not constructed for the purposes of MatrixRTC (it may still be constructed for Synapse and/or Hookshot).
